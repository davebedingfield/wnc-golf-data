The oldest golf course in Polk County, established in 1914. Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, the 1922 Rustic Revival log clubhouse is one of the most intact examples of early mountain golf culture in the Southeast. Tree-lined Bermuda fairways feed into small, undulating bentgrass greens. Tom Jackson’s 9-hole par-3 executive course at Willow Valley Resort sits in a quiet mountain valley just five minutes from Boone. Known locally as “the little green monster,” the layout plays deceptively challenging with four tee boxes per hole, significant elevation changes, and yardages ranging from 137 to 221 yards. It draws a friendly mix of resort guests, families, and High Country regulars looking for a fast, affordable round without the pretense of a big-club experience.Don’t let the “par-3” label fool you — because of the drastic mountain elevation changes, holes will play significantly longer or shorter than the scorecard reads. The signature 9th hole plays 221 yards from the tips and is a legit test at elevation.
9-hole par-3 course; not USGA rated

W.B. Lewis’s 18-hole mountain course from 1965 rises from 3,600 to 4,800 feet of elevation, making it one of the highest courses east of the Mississippi. At this altitude, the ball flies noticeably farther through the thin mountain air, requiring a full recalibration of club selection.
